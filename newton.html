Catawba County

Why people love Newton

Newton is the county seat of Catawba County, just south of Conover and minutes from Hickory. Its historic 1924 courthouse anchors a walkable downtown, and the town is known for Soldiers' Reunion, one of the longest-running community celebrations in the country.

  • Historic downtown and courthouse square
  • Soldiers' Reunion every August
  • Established neighborhoods and good value
  • Easy access to US-321, I-40 and NC-16
