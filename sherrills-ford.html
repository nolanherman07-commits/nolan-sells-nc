Why people love Sherrills Ford

Sherrills Ford is a lakeside community in southeastern Catawba County on Lake Norman's northwest shore. It's popular with buyers who want lake life, golf and bigger lots, with Hickory, Mooresville and Charlotte all within reach.

  • Waterfront and lake-access homes
  • Golf and lake communities
  • Bigger lots and a quieter pace
  • Between Hickory, Mooresville and Charlotte
